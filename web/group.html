      Group ride

      Follow several riders on one map. Paste each rider's live link, one per line. Karoo Live links show full numbers; KarooTracker ids show position. Links from other trackers (Garmin LiveTrack, Wahoo, Strava Beacon…) are listed with a button to open them.

      For another tracker's link, put the rider's name on the same line. You can also paste an existing group link to add to it.

        The group lives entirely in the link: there's no account and nothing is stored. Each rider's data stays in their own database.
